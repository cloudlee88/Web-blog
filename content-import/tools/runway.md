## Overview

Runway is an AI video platform offering text-to-video and video-to-video generation plus a suite of "magic tools" (inpainting, background removal, motion, and more) aimed at creators and studios.

## What it's good at

Breadth and momentum. It bundles the widest set of AI video capabilities in one app, and the models improve on a steady cadence. For short clips, B-roll and effects, the output is genuinely usable.

## Where it falls short

High-quality generations consume credits quickly, so costs add up. It's built for short clips rather than long-form video, and you'll still generate several takes to get a keeper.

## Should you use it?

Creators making short social/ad video, effects or concept clips will get real value. If you need long-form editing, use Runway for the AI shots and assemble them elsewhere.
