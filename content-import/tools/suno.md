## Overview

Suno generates complete songs — lyrics, vocals and instrumentation — from a text prompt describing the style, mood and topic.

## What it's good at

Turning an idea into an actual song, fast. It's excellent for background tracks, jingles, hooks and demos, and iterating across genres is quick and genuinely fun. The free tier is enough to get a feel for it.

## Where it falls short

Commercial usage rights depend on your plan, so read the licence before publishing. You get less granular control than a real DAW, and quality can vary between takes — expect to regenerate.

## Should you use it?

For creators who need original background music, jingles or song demos without a studio, Suno is the best of the current crop. Musicians wanting precise control will still reach for their DAW.
