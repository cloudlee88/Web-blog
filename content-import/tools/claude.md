## Overview

Claude is Anthropic's AI assistant, known for a large context window, a natural writing voice and strong reasoning on complex prompts.

## What it's good at

Two things stand out. First, context: you can paste in very long documents or codebases and ask questions across all of it. Second, tone — Claude's default writing reads less like marketing copy than many alternatives, which matters if you publish what it helps you draft.

## Where it falls short

Its ecosystem of third-party integrations is smaller than ChatGPT's, and image generation isn't its focus. Availability and limits vary by region and plan.

## Should you use it?

If your work is writing-heavy or involves large documents and code, Claude is the one to try first. Many people run it alongside ChatGPT and pick per task.
