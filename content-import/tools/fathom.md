## Overview

Fathom is an AI meeting notetaker that records, transcribes and summarises calls, with a notably generous free plan and a lean toward sales/CRM workflows.

## What it's good at

Value and clarity. The free plan covers unlimited recording and AI summaries, and the summaries are fast and well-structured with clear action items. You can flag highlights live, then share or sync them — including to a CRM on paid tiers.

## Where it falls short

Auto-joining calls needs calendar access, team and CRM features are paid, and — as with any recorder — you're responsible for consent.

## Should you use it?

If you want a reliable meeting notetaker without paying, Fathom is the one to start with. Sales teams wanting CRM sync and sharing will get value from the paid tiers.
