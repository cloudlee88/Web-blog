## Overview

Ideogram is an AI image generator with a standout strength: rendering legible, well-placed text inside images — the thing most generators still struggle with.

## What it's good at

Text. Posters, logos, ad creative and social graphics that need actual words come out far cleaner than on rival tools. General image quality is strong too, and the free tier lets you test it properly.

## Where it falls short

For pure artistic quality, Midjourney still edges ahead. Longer text strings can need a retry, and there are fewer advanced controls than power users may want.

## Should you use it?

If your images need readable text — captions, posters, logos — Ideogram is the best pick. For maximum artistry without text, compare against Midjourney.
