## Overview

Creative Market is a marketplace where independent designers sell fonts, graphics, templates, themes and other digital assets on a buy-once basis.

## What it's good at

Character and ownership. The assets tend to be higher-quality and more distinctive than generic stock, and you keep what you buy under a perpetual licence. Weekly free goods are a nice way to build a library over time.

## Where it falls short

Buying per item adds up if you need a lot — there's no unlimited subscription. Quality and licence terms vary from shop to shop, so read each listing.

## Should you use it?

For occasional, high-quality, distinctive design assets you want to own, Creative Market is ideal. For high-volume downloading, a subscription like Envato Elements is cheaper.
