## Overview

Recraft is an AI image tool aimed at designers, with reusable brand styles and — unusually — true vector (SVG) output alongside raster images.

## What it's good at

Design consistency. You can define a style and reuse it across a whole set of assets, keeping everything on-brand, and export scalable vectors for icons and logos. Text and icon handling are strong.

## Where it falls short

For pure artistic wow, Midjourney still leads. Setting up custom styles takes some effort, and the best features (vector export, custom styles) need a paid plan.

## Should you use it?

For designers and brand teams who need consistent, reusable, scalable assets, Recraft is the standout. For one-off artistic images, compare with Midjourney or Ideogram.
