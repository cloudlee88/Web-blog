## Overview

Luma's Dream Machine generates video from text prompts or still images, with a reputation for smooth, natural motion.

## What it's good at

Motion. It handles movement more convincingly than many rivals, and it's particularly good at animating a still image into a short living clip. The interface is approachable and free credits let you test it.

## Where it falls short

Clips are short, credits go quickly on the free tier, and prompt adherence can vary — expect to regenerate for the exact shot you want.

## Should you use it?

For animating images and producing short, good-looking motion clips, it's a strong choice. For a broad video toolkit, compare with Runway; for playful quick clips, Pika.
