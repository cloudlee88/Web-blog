## Overview

Copy.ai started as an AI copywriting tool and has expanded into automated go-to-market (GTM) workflows for sales and marketing teams, on top of its template library.

## What it's good at

Short-form speed. Ads, social captions, product descriptions and outreach snippets come quickly, and the free tier lets you trial it properly. The workflow features can chain steps for repetitive, high-volume tasks.

## Where it falls short

Long-form drafts still read like drafts — plan to edit. The newer workflow builder is powerful but takes time to learn, and the short-form copy space is crowded with cheaper options.

## Should you use it?

Marketing and sales teams doing high-volume short-form copy or building repeatable content workflows will get the most from it. For occasional writing, a general assistant is cheaper.
