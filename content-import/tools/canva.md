## Overview

Canva is a browser-based design tool built around templates and drag-and-drop, with a growing suite of AI features (background removal, image generation, magic write and more) under Magic Studio.

## What it's good at

Accessibility. Anyone can make presentations, social posts and simple graphics that look professional, fast. The template quality is high, and the AI tools remove tedious steps like cutting out backgrounds.

## Where it falls short

Professional designers will find it limiting versus dedicated tools, and template-based work can look generic if you don't customise. Some of the best assets and AI credits sit behind Pro.

## Should you use it?

For solopreneurs, marketers and teams without a designer, it's a no-brainer. Pro pays for itself if you publish visuals regularly.
