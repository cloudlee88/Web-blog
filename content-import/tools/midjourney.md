## Overview

Midjourney generates images from text prompts and is widely regarded as producing the most aesthetically polished output of the mainstream generators.

## What it's good at

Out of the box, Midjourney images look art-directed — lighting, composition and mood tend to land without much prompt engineering. Recent versions handle coherence and text-in-image far better than early releases.

## Where it falls short

The workflow runs largely through Discord, which feels alien if you're used to a normal web app (a web interface has been rolling out). There's no meaningful free tier, and precise control over exact layouts still takes effort.

## Should you use it?

For marketing visuals, concept art and social content where look-and-feel matters most, it's the top pick. If you need pixel-precise control or a free option, look at alternatives too.
