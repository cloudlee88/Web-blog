## Overview

ElevenLabs turns text into remarkably natural speech and can clone voices from short samples. It's popular for video narration, audiobooks and dubbing.

## What it's good at

Realism. The intonation and pacing are noticeably ahead of older TTS, and multilingual output plus dubbing open up international content. The API makes it easy to build into your own pipeline.

## Where it falls short

Higher-quality generation eats your monthly character quota quickly, and voice cloning raises real consent and licensing questions you have to manage responsibly. Fine emotional control still takes iteration.

## Should you use it?

For creators producing narration, dubbing or voiceover at volume, it's the top choice. Casual users can do a lot on the free tier first.
